      05

      Access & sensory guide

      An old power station can be loud, dark and big. Here's what to expect and how we help.

      • Step-free

        Lifts reach levels 0 to 2. The roof terrace is stairs only, so we show its view on screens in the café.

      • Quiet hour

        Sundays 10:00–11:00: sound installations off, lights steady, half capacity.

      • Ear defenders

        Free to borrow at the desk and at the Turbine Hall entrance. Signal / Noise peaks at 85 dB.

      • Flashing lights

        Line of Sight uses strobe. It's in a closed room with a warning at the door, so you can skip it easily.

      • Companions go free

        Bring a carer or companion at no cost. Add them at the desk; no proof needed.

      • Seats everywhere

        Folding stools at every entrance, and benches in each gallery.

      Thursdays are free after five.

      Late opening until 21:00, with a bar in the Turbine Hall. Book a free slot so you don't queue.
